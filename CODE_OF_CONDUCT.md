# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive collaboration environment.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Give actionable feedback
- Focus on problems, not people
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Repeated disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take further action to protect the project environment.
